## The problem

A KPI on its own ("revenue: $1.2M") doesn't tell you much. Tableau Metrics users wanted to know right away whether a number is **better or worse than before**, on their phone as well as at their desk.

## What I built

On Tableau's Metrics team I developed a feature that compares a KPI against its historical data, for both the mobile app and the desktop/server experience. Users can now author metrics that include historical comparisons.

- Worked across the stack: Kotlin, C#, TypeScript, React/Redux and React Native.
- Shipped in Scrum sprints with Azure DevOps.
