## The team

YouTube's Main App Web team builds the desktop web experience: the watch page, home, search and everything around them. I worked there for two years doing full-stack development.

## Comment teaser on the watch page

On desktop, comments live far below the video. Many viewers never scroll to them, so a big part of what makes a video feel alive goes unseen. I built and deployed a **comment teaser**: a short preview of the conversation shown below the video details, so it's visible without leaving the video.
