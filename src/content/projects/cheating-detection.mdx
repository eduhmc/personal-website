## The problem

With more than 700 students and remote exams, course staff had no scalable way to keep assessments fair. Reusing questions invited sharing, and reviewing submissions by hand didn't scale.

## What I built

- A **cheating-detection system** that used data science techniques to flag suspicious submission patterns for staff to review.
- **Question generators** that create variants of exam questions, so each student gets a different version.

## Who it was for

The "users" were course staff, so the bar was trust: flags had to be explainable enough for a TA to act on.
