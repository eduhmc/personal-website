## The problem

On desktop, comments live far below the video. Many viewers never scroll to them, so a big part of what makes a video feel alive goes unseen.

## What I did

I built and deployed a **comment teaser** on the YouTube watch page: a short preview of the conversation shown below the video details, so it's visible without leaving the video.
